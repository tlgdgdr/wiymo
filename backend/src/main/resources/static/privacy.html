1. Who we are

SocialWorld ("the App") is operated by [COMPANY] ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What we collect

We collect only what the App needs to work. We never see your password — it is stored as a one-way cryptographic hash.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the App: accounts, profiles, rooms, chat, gifts and discovery.
  • To keep the community safe: enforcing blocks, reviewing reports, and suspending accounts that break our Terms of Service.
  • To notify you: push notifications for messages and gifts, if you opt in.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who can see what

  • Other users can see your username, age (not your date of birth), country, bio, languages, avatar, current intention, online status and room presence.
  • Never visible to other users: your email address, date of birth, and password hash.
  • Our moderation team can access reported content, including messages, to review reports.

5. Service providers

We share limited data with providers that host and operate the App:

  • Hosting and database providers that store the data described above.
  • Expo (Expo Push Notification service), which receives your push token and notification content in order to deliver notifications.

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete my account), your profile is anonymized immediately: your username, email, bio, avatar, languages and push token are removed or replaced with anonymous values, and all of your sessions are revoked. Messages you sent remain visible to their recipients but are attributed to an anonymized account.
  • Reports are retained as long as necessary for safety and legal purposes.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and Turkish KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can:

  • Edit your profile, languages and avatar at any time inside the App.
  • Delete your account yourself from Settings → Delete my account.
  • Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] for any other request. We respond within 30 days.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Age requirement

The App is for adults only. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, and we verify this at registration using your date of birth. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are stored using BCrypt hashing; sign-in tokens are short-lived and rotated; traffic to our servers uses TLS. No system is perfectly secure — if we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by law.

10. International transfers

Our servers are located in [JURISDICTION/REGION]. If you use the App from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and processed in that region under appropriate safeguards.
